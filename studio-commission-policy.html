Sales Commission Policy — Studio Team

STRONG Pilates Bankstown, Castle Hill, Leppington, Wetherill Park and Penrith — Effective 2 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to trainers, front of house and customer care staff at STRONG Pilates Bankstown, Castle Hill, Leppington, Wetherill Park and Penrith.

It sets out what you are paid when you sell a membership or pack. It does not change your pay rate or the terms of your employment or contractor agreement.

This commission is a trial. It will be reviewed to check that it is sustainable for the business and practical for the team day to day. See section 10.

The Membership Sales Consultant is paid under a separate policy. Their commission does not reduce yours, except for packs as set out in section 2.

2. What earns commission and how much

SaleCommission

No commission is paid on intro offers, casual classes, changes to an existing membership, or a membership that is put back on after being suspended.

3. When a sale qualifies

Within the studio team, one person is credited per sale; sales are not split between studio team members. The Membership Sales Consultant may also be credited on the same sale under their own policy (see section 1). A customer who leaves without signing up is not yet a sale. A membership or pack qualifies for you in the cases listed under your role below.

5. When a sale counts

A sale counts in the month the customer's first payment is received. A membership that is set up but not yet paid is pending, not a sale.

The monthly bonus is counted on approved memberships with a first payment in that calendar month.

6. Recording sales

Every sale is entered in the Studio Sales Tracker by the person credited, as soon as the sign-up is confirmed: date, customer, studio, package and how it qualified.

A sale that is not in the tracker when the month is approved is not paid for that month.

7. How and when commission is paid

Commission is calculated monthly from the tracker and the studio system, reviewed and approved by the Sales Director, and paid two to three weeks after the end of the month.

Employees are paid through payroll. Contractors add the approved amount to their next invoice.

8. Cancellations and refunds

If a sale is cancelled or refunded before the month is approved, no commission is paid on it.

If a membership is cancelled or refunded after commission has been paid and the member did not complete four weeks, the commission is deducted from your next commission payment.

If a pack is refunded after commission has been paid, the commission is deducted from your next commission payment.

The monthly bonus is not deducted once paid.

9. Disputes

If you believe a sale has been credited incorrectly, raise it in writing with the Sales Director within seven days of the monthly commission list being issued. It will be decided from the tracker and the studio system. The Sales Director's decision is final.

10. Changes to this policy

This commission is a trial. The policy will be reviewed after three months to check that it is sustainable for the business and practical for the team day to day. It may be changed, including the amounts and the addition of other bonuses, with two weeks' notice given through any of the company's communication channels (for example email, WhatsApp or a team meeting). Changes apply to sales made from the date they take effect.
